Pricing

As a Quaker, I believe in plain dealing — speaking truthfully, keeping commitments, and treating everyone fairly. For me, this means my prices are set honestly and openly from the start.


  • No hidden fees. What you see is what you pay.
  • No negotiation games. Bargaining often favors those who argue hardest, not those with the greatest need. I want everyone to be treated equally.
  • Honest value. My rates reflect the real time and care I put into the work, without inflation or gimmicks.


When I say a project costs a certain amount, it’s because I’ve carefully estimated the hours it will take at $30/hour. That way, both of us know exactly what’s being offered and expected.


This is more than business — it’s integrity. You can trust that I won’t charge one person more just because they can pay more, and I won’t undercut myself just to “win” a deal. We meet on level ground.
